How It Works

A simple repair process designed to be clear and convenient

  1. Step 1

    Submit a repair request.

  2. Step 2

    I'll review the issue and contact you.

  3. Step 3

    In most cases, I'll schedule a pickup time for your device.

  4. Step 4

    I'll diagnose, repair, and thoroughly test it.

  5. Step 5

    I'll safely return your repaired device.

Home Visit Policy

Most repairs are completed through device pickup and return, allowing me to work in a proper environment with the tools I need.

Some situations, such as printer setup or internet-related issues, may occasionally require an in-home visit. These requests are reviewed case by case and are only available when appropriate and with my parent's approval.

If you believe your issue cannot be solved through pickup, you may request an exception in the repair form. Please note that most repairs will still be completed off-site.

FAQ

Common questions about the service

Is this really free?

Yes. Ctrl Alt Help is a free volunteer service for Calusa Park residents.

Who can request repairs?

Only residents of Calusa Park can request service.

What devices do you repair?

I focus on computers, laptops, printers, and other portable technology-related devices.

Do you come inside homes?

Pickup is the standard process. Limited exceptions may be available depending on the repair and with parental approval.

How long do repairs usually take?

That depends on the issue, but I will communicate clearly about timing after I review the request.

Why are you doing this?

I created this service to give back to the Calusa Park community while completing my NJHS volunteer hours and helping people feel more comfortable with technology.
